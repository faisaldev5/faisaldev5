## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Faisal Khan**, a **MERN Stack & WordPress Developer** focused on building modern, responsive, scalable, and production-ready web solutions.

I work across the complete development lifecycle: planning, frontend development, backend systems, databases, APIs, third-party integrations, deployment, hosting, performance optimization, and ongoing maintenance.

My core stack includes **React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, JavaScript, TypeScript, WordPress, PHP, and SQL**. I also work with modern deployment and infrastructure workflows across **VPS environments, AWS, Vercel, Cloudflare, and Linux-based servers**.

I'm also the **Co-Founder of [SparkDevX](https://sparkdevx.com)**, where we design and develop digital solutions for businesses and clients.

I care about choosing the right technology for the problem, keeping implementation clean, and shipping products that work reliably in the real world.
